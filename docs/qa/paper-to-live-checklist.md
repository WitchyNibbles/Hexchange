# Paper To Live Checklist

- Strategy passed promotion gates with a sufficient sample size.
- Strategy has a real Nautilus backtest, not only seeded simulation output.
- Fee-adjusted returns remain positive after modeled slippage.
- Paper-session drift versus backtest is within tolerance.
- Paper session is active and reporting heartbeats in the Strategy library.
- Kill switch was tested successfully.
- Live allocation cap is set to a tiny rollout size.
- Control Center live readiness report shows venue blockers clearly.
- Broker paper and live credentials are stored separately.
- Event log persists across restarts.
- Operator can explain why the strategy is entering or exiting.
- Interactive Brokers is connected before stock live trading.
- Kraken is connected before crypto live trading.
